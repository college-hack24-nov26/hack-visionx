# Food Waste Saver - Home Page Text

## Main heading
Good food should never go to waste.

## Line below the heading
Food Waste Saver connects homes, hotels and events that have extra food with nearby people and NGOs who can use it - before it expires.

## Buttons
- Post surplus food
- Browse food

## How it works
1. **Post it** - A donor adds the food, portions, pickup place and a deadline within 24 hours.
2. **Claim it** - A nearby person or NGO claims the portions they can collect and gets a secret pickup code.
3. **Pick it up** - The donor checks the code, the food is collected, and nothing goes to waste.

## Food safety line
Every donor must tick a food-safety checklist before posting, and every listing shows its deadline clearly.

## Impact line
Every collected portion is counted on our Impact Wall, so the whole community can see how much food was saved together.
