# Food Waste Saver - Dish Descriptions

## Batch 1

- **Idli** - Soft, steamed rice cakes. Light and easy to eat, usually served with sambar and chutney.
- **Samosa** - Crisp fried pastry with a spiced potato filling. A popular tea-time snack.
- **Misal Pav** - Spicy sprouted-bean curry topped with crunchy farsan and onion, served with pav (soft bread rolls). A Maharashtrian favourite.
- **Pav Bhaji** - Mixed vegetables mashed and cooked with butter and spices, served hot with buttered pav.
- **Poha** - Flattened rice cooked with onion, turmeric and peanuts. A light breakfast dish.
- **Upma** - Rava (semolina) cooked soft with vegetables and mild spices. A simple breakfast dish.
- **Dhokla** - Soft, spongy steamed gram-flour cake, light and mildly spiced. A Gujarati snack.
- **Masala Dosa** - Crisp rice crepe with a spiced potato filling, served with sambar and chutney.
- **Vada Pav** - A spiced potato fritter placed inside a pav with chutneys. Famous Mumbai street food.
- **Puri Bhaji** - Puffed fried puris served with a spiced potato vegetable.

## Batch 2

- **Veg Biryani** - Fragrant long-grain rice cooked with mixed vegetables and whole spices, served hot, often with raita.
- **Chole Bhature** - Spicy chickpea curry served with big, fluffy deep-fried bhature breads. A Punjabi favourite.
- **Paneer Butter Masala** - Soft paneer cubes in a rich, creamy tomato-butter gravy. Best with naan or rice.
- **Dal Tadka** - Yellow lentils cooked soft and finished with a hot ghee, garlic and cumin tempering. Served with rice or roti.
- **Jeera Rice** - Plain basmati rice gently flavoured with cumin seeds and ghee. Goes with any curry or dal.
- **Khichdi** - Rice and lentils cooked together into a soft, comforting one-pot meal, often served with ghee and pickle.
- **Aloo Paratha** - Whole-wheat flatbread stuffed with spiced mashed potato, served hot with curd or pickle.
- **Medu Vada** - Crisp outside, soft inside fried lentil rings, served with sambar and coconut chutney.
- **Sabudana Khichdi** - Sago pearls cooked with peanuts, potato and mild spices. A Maharashtrian fasting-day favourite.
- **Veg Kolhapuri** - Mixed vegetables in a spicy, fiery Kolhapuri masala gravy. Served with roti or rice.

## Batch 3

- **Modak** - Sweet rice-flour dumplings filled with coconut and jaggery. Lord Ganesh's favourite in Maharashtra.
- **Gulab Jamun** - Soft, warm milk dumplings soaked in sugar syrup. A celebration sweet across India.
- **Jalebi** - Crisp golden spirals soaked in sugar syrup, best served hot.
- **Rasgulla** - Soft, spongy cheese balls in light sugar syrup. A famous Bengali sweet.
- **Shrikhand** - Thick, creamy sweet curd flavoured with saffron and cardamom, served chilled.
- **Puran Poli** - Soft flatbread stuffed with a sweet lentil-and-jaggery filling, served with ghee. A Maharashtrian festive dish.
- **Kheer** - Slow-cooked rice pudding made with milk, cardamom and nuts, served warm or chilled.
- **Mysore Pak** - A rich ghee and gram-flour sweet from Mysuru that melts in the mouth.
- **Soan Papdi** - Light, flaky sweet cubes with a gentle cardamom flavour, often shared at festivals.
- **Aloo Tikki** - Crisp spiced potato patties, served with chutneys as a popular street snack.
