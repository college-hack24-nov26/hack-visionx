# Judge Questions - Practice Sheet

Short, honest answers for desk visits. If a judge asks a deep code question, smile and say: "Prathamesh built that part - he will explain it best", and call him over. Never claim a feature the app does not have, and never guess numbers.

**Q1. What problem does your app solve?**
Good food from homes, hotels and functions gets thrown away, while people nearby go hungry. Our app connects the two before the food's time runs out.

**Q2. Who can post food?**
Any registered user - a home, a hotel, a hostel mess, or an event organiser with leftover food.

**Q3. Who can claim food?**
Individuals who need it, and NGOs. NGOs have their own type of account so donors can recognise them.

**Q4. How does the pickup happen?**
The claimer gets a secret pickup code. At handover, the donor checks that code - only the right person collects the food.

**Q5. What if nobody claims the food in time?**
The listing shows its deadline clearly, the app sends alerts as the deadline comes close, and expired food is removed automatically so nobody is misled.

**Q6. How do you keep the food safe?**
Before posting, the donor confirms the food is fresh and stored properly. Every listing has a pickup deadline, and we advise claimers to check the food before eating.

**Q7. Is the donor's phone number public?**
No. It is shown only if the donor allows it, and only to signed-in users - never to visitors.

**Q8. Does it work in Marathi and Hindi?**
The home page text and all the main button and menu words are ready in Marathi and Hindi - our team prepared them during the event.

**Q9. What did your team build here, during the event?**
Everything in this project: accounts, food listings, browsing, claims with pickup codes, expiry alerts and notifications - built step by step since the event started. The commits in our repository show the work as it happened.

**Q10. How is this better than a WhatsApp group?**
A group message gets buried and forgotten. Here every listing has portions, a deadline, and a claim record with a pickup code - so food is tracked until it reaches someone.
